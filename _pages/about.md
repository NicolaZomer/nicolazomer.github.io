Ciao! I am a PhD student in computer science at Sorbonne University, within <a href="https://www.isir.upmc.fr/" target="_blank">ISIR</a> and the <a href="https://giuliademasi.weebly.com/meera-research-lab.html" target="_blank">MEERA research group</a>, working under the supervision of <a href="https://giuliademasi.weebly.com/" target="_blank">Giulia De Masi</a> and <a href="https://scholar.google.fr/citations?user=a5JvBbgAAAAJ&hl=fr" target="_blank">Antonio Celani</a>. The project is funded by <a href="https://marine-weather.com/" target="_blank">Marine Weather Intelligence</a>, a French deeptech startup building AI-driven solutions to improve routing, weather forecasting, and maritime safety.

My research spans ocean and atmosphere modeling, multi-objective weather routing, and ship model calibration, all tackled with a data-driven approach that relies on forecast, historical, and sensor data. I am particularly interested in incorporating uncertainty and expert knowledge into navigation systems, for example to minimize the risk of routing vessels into hazardous weather.

During my Master's in Physics of Data, I also worked on multi-agent systems, in particular LLM swarms, and on generative network models, which I applied to understand the dynamics of platforms in the Fediverse.

Beyond my academic and professional pursuits, I am also a passionate handbalancer! This unique discipline allows me to deeply experience the mechanisms of human learning, which I find extremely fascinating.
